Tutorial


  1. Click the puzzle piece at the top right of the screen.
    • If you cannot find it, select your profile picture instead and click "extensions".
  2. Click on the caterpillar icon to open the extension's settings.
    • If you'd like easier access to the menu, consider pinning the extension to your toolbar using the pin icon.
  3. Tweak the values to your liking.
    • These values will save upon closing the page. No need to worry!
    • If you'd like some background images to use, download some I made here.
  4. Open or reload the OB Digital Platform!

(Please note that as of version , extensions are sadly not allowed on school accounts.)
